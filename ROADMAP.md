# Roadmap

## 0.1.0 (09.10.2026)
- Overview with verdict, Apps, Battery, History, Why slow?, Settings; live status notification with the
  value as status bar icon; Quick Settings tile; alerts (heat, storage, weak charger, power-hungry app)
- Precise battery with sessions, widgets, hardware page, RAM split, pro mode on demand (Shizuku or computer grant)
- No internet permission; eight languages; tested in the Android 15 emulator

## 0.2.0 (09.10.2026)
- Background battery per app every day with the computer grant; charge alarms, charger test, capacity trend; weekly report; interrupted-measuring card; VPN apps not flagged

## 0.3.0 (10.10.2026)
- Storage page: breakdown, growth and forecast, caches, unused apps, media check with duplicates, speed test

## 0.4.0 (18.10.2026)
- Wakeups and wakelocks per app, battery passport, power per component, charging as a Live Update; optional read grants

## 0.5.0 (19.10.2026)
- Night check, Google's wakelock limit, planned alarms and jobs, sensor log, since the update, heat and throttling, forecast by pattern, charging habits, cost per hour, share/export, two widgets

## 0.6.0 (21.10.2026)
- Today at a glance, grouped and foldable cards, no silent gaps, Activity+'s own cost, landscape/tablet, import, accessibility, reviewed translations
